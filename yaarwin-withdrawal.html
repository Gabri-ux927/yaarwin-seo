💰 How to Withdraw Money from Yaarwin

Withdraw your winnings instantly to your bank account or UPI ID. Follow these simple steps.

📋 Withdrawal Requirements

Minimum Withdrawal: ₹110
Maximum Withdrawal: No limit (VIP players get higher limits)
Processing Time: 2-5 minutes
Fees: Zero fees on all withdrawals

📱 Step-by-Step Withdrawal Process

Step 1: Login to your Yaarwin account
Step 2: Click on "Wallet" in the bottom menu
Step 3: Select "Withdraw" button
Step 4: Enter amount (minimum ₹110)
Step 5: Select payment method (UPI or Bank Transfer)
Step 6: Enter your UPI ID or bank details
Step 7: Click "Confirm" and wait 2-5 minutes
⚠️ Important: Always verify your UPI ID or bank details before confirming. Wrong details will delay your withdrawal.
