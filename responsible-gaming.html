🎗️ Responsible Gaming at Yaarwin

Yaarwin is committed to promoting responsible gaming practices for all players.

📋 Our Responsible Gaming Principles

Play for Entertainment: Treat colour trading as entertainment, not a source of income.
Set a Budget: Decide how much you can afford to lose before you start playing.
Take Breaks: Step away regularly. Don't play for hours continuously.
Never Chase Losses: Trying to recover losses leads to bigger losses.
18+ Only: You must be 18 years or older to play on Yaarwin.

⚠️ Warning Signs of Problem Gaming

• Spending more than you can afford
• Borrowing money to play
• Neglecting work or family
• Lying about time or money spent
• Chasing losses repeatedly
